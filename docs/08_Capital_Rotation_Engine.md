# AI Fleet Commander
# Capital Rotation Engine

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Capital Rotation Engine is responsible for dynamically moving portfolio capital toward the highest-quality opportunities while protecting the portfolio from unnecessary concentration and risk.

Capital is not permanently assigned to any strategy or bot.

Every allocation must be continuously earned.

---

# Philosophy

Money follows opportunity.

Not bots.

Not strategies.

Not emotions.

Capital moves continuously as market conditions change.

---

# Responsibilities

The Capital Rotation Engine continuously:

- Evaluates every active bot
- Reviews current allocations
- Detects stronger opportunities
- Rotates capital
- Maintains portfolio balance
- Protects portfolio liquidity
- Controls reserve cash

---

# Capital Pools

Portfolio capital is divided into:

Trading Capital

Reserve Capital

Emergency Capital

Profit Reserve

Expansion Capital

---

# Rotation Triggers

Rotate capital when:

- Higher ranked bot appears
- Market regime changes
- Bot confidence falls
- Risk increases
- Better opportunity detected
- Portfolio exposure becomes excessive
