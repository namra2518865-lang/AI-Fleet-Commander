# AI Fleet Commander
## System Architecture
Version: 0.1

---

# Vision

AI Fleet Commander is an AI Portfolio Management System designed to supervise multiple independent crypto trading bots.

It does not generate trades.

It does not replace trading strategies.

Its primary responsibility is to understand the market, evaluate every strategy continuously, rank all available bots, allocate capital dynamically, control portfolio risk, and maximize long-term portfolio performance.

Claude acts as the Chief Investment Officer (CIO) of the trading system.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every trading bot has strengths.

Every trading bot has weaknesses.

No strategy wins in every market.

The objective is not to build one perfect strategy.

The objective is to deploy the correct strategy at the correct time.

The Fleet Commander exists to answer one question continuously:

"Which strategy deserves capital right now?"

---

# Responsibilities

Fleet Commander is responsible for:

• Market Analysis

• Market Regime Detection

• Strategy Evaluation

• Bot Ranking

• Opportunity Evaluation

• Capital Allocation

• Portfolio Risk

• Exposure Control

• Position Monitoring

• Performance Monitoring

• Daily Learning

---

# Responsibilities of Trading Bots

Trading bots are NOT controlled by Claude.

Trading bots remain independent.

Each bot:

- Generates signals
- Executes its own strategy
- Manages its own positions

Fleet Commander only decides:

Should this bot receive capital?

Should this opportunity be approved?

Should this bot receive priority?
