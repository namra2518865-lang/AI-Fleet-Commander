# AI Fleet Commander
# Opportunity Engine

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity Engine is responsible for evaluating every trade opportunity generated by all bots.

Bots generate opportunities.

The Opportunity Engine decides whether those opportunities deserve portfolio capital.

---

# Philosophy

Not every signal deserves capital.

The best opportunity wins.

Capital follows quality.

---

# Responsibilities

The Opportunity Engine continuously:

- Collects opportunities from all bots
- Scores every opportunity
- Evaluates market alignment
- Reviews portfolio impact
- Calculates opportunity quality
- Sends recommendations to the Portfolio Manager

---

# Opportunity Pipeline

Bot Signal

↓

Market Validation

↓

Risk Review

↓

Portfolio Review

↓

Scoring

↓

Approval / Rejection

---

# Approval Rules

Approve only if:

- Market regime supports the strategy
- Confidence > 70
- Risk acceptable
- Portfolio heat acceptable
- Capital available

---

# Rejection Rules

Reject immediately if:

- Risk too high
- Correlation too high
- News danger
- Portfolio overloaded
- Drawdown limit reached

---

# Queue System

The Opportunity Engine maintains:

High Priority Queue

Medium Priority Queue

Watchlist Queue

Rejected Queue

---

# Capital Competition

Multiple bots may compete for the same capital.

Example:

Bot 3 = Score 92

Bot 11 = Score 90

Bot 9 = Score 87

Bot 5 = Score 81

Capital is allocated by score.

---

# Portfolio Impact

The engine evaluates:

- Coin concentration
- Sector concentration
- Correlation
- Existing exposure
- Reserve capital

---

# AI Review

Bot 11 may:

- Suggest upgrades
- Suggest downgrades
- Suggest delays

Bot 11 cannot approve trades.

---

# Emergency Rules

Pause opportunity approvals if:

- Emergency mode active
- Flash crash
- Exchange outage
- Extreme volatility

---

# Final Rule

Signals create opportunities.

Opportunities compete.

Only the strongest survive.

---

# Conclusion

The Opportunity Engine transforms raw trading signals into intelligent portfolio opportunities.

It acts as the selection mechanism of AI Fleet Commander.
