# AI Fleet Commander
## Project Vision

Version: 1.0

---

# Executive Summary

AI Fleet Commander is an institutional-grade AI Portfolio Management System designed to supervise, evaluate, coordinate, and optimize multiple independent cryptocurrency trading bots.

The Fleet Commander does not replace trading strategies.

Instead, it continuously studies the market, understands changing conditions, evaluates every strategy, ranks trading bots, allocates capital dynamically, manages portfolio risk, and maximizes long-term portfolio performance.

Claude acts as the Chief Investment Officer (CIO) of the entire trading ecosystem.

---

# Mission

Create an intelligent AI system capable of making portfolio-level decisions instead of individual trade decisions.

The objective is to ensure that every dollar of capital is allocated to the highest-probability opportunities while maintaining strict portfolio risk management.

---

# Vision

Build one of the world's most adaptive AI trading management systems capable of:

- Monitoring markets 24/7
- Supervising multiple trading bots simultaneously
- Understanding changing market conditions
- Dynamically ranking strategies
- Allocating capital intelligently
- Reducing unnecessary risk
- Continuously improving portfolio performance

The Fleet Commander should behave like a professional hedge fund manager rather than a simple trading bot.

---

# Core Philosophy

Markets change continuously.

Strategies do not.

Therefore,

the system must continuously determine:

> Which strategy deserves capital right now?

The Fleet Commander never falls in love with any strategy.

Every strategy must earn capital through performance, market alignment, and opportunity quality.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Fleet Commander exists to:

• Understand the market.

• Detect market regimes.

• Evaluate every trading strategy.

• Rank all trading bots.

• Allocate capital dynamically.

- Predicts the future
- Overrides exchange rules

# Trading Bot Architecture

Fleet Commander supervises multiple independent bots.

Each bot:

- Uses its own strategy.
- Generates its own signals.
- Manages its own positions.

Fleet Commander only decides:

- Which bot receives capital?
- Which opportunity should be approved?
- Which strategy should receive priority?
