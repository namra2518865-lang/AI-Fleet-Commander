# AI Fleet Commander
# Risk Engine

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Risk Engine protects portfolio capital.

No strategy, bot, or opportunity may bypass risk controls.

Risk management has higher authority than profitability.

---

# Philosophy

Protect capital first.

Grow capital second.

Survival always comes before returns.

---

# Responsibilities

The Risk Engine continuously:

- Monitors portfolio risk
- Calculates exposure
- Controls leverage
- Monitors drawdowns
- Evaluates correlations
- Detects emergencies
- Limits losses
- Approves or rejects opportunities

---

# Risk Hierarchy

Portfolio Risk

↓

↓

↓

Trade Risk

No lower layer may override a higher layer.

# Correlation Rules

Avoid excessive exposure to:

- Highly correlated coins
- Same market sectors
- Similar strategies

Maximum correlated exposure:

40%

---

# Futures Rules

Reduce allocation when:

- Funding becomes extreme
- Liquidation risk increases
- Volatility spikes

---

# Emergency Triggers

Activate emergency mode when:

- Flash crash
- Exchange outage
- Stablecoin depeg
- Extreme liquidation event
- Black swan news

---

# Emergency Actions

Emergency mode may:

- Reduce exposure
- Pause bots
- Increase cash reserve
- Block new trades
