# AI Fleet Commander
# AI Committee

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Committee is the governing body of AI Fleet Commander.

Instead of relying on a single AI opinion, Fleet Commander uses multiple specialized AI roles to evaluate markets, strategies, risk, and capital allocation.

The final decision is made only after all committee members submit their recommendations.

---

# Philosophy

No single model should control the portfolio.

Every decision must be reviewed from multiple perspectives.

---

# Committee Structure

Chief Market Officer (CMO)

↓

Chief Strategy Officer (CSO)

↓

Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

↓

Chief Capital Officer (CCO)

↓

Chief Portfolio Officer (CPO)

↓

Chief Execution Officer (CEO)

---

# Chief Market Officer (CMO)

Responsibilities:

- Analyze market structure
- Detect market regime
- Monitor volatility
- Monitor liquidity
- Evaluate sentiment

Questions:

- What market are we in?
- Is the trend healthy?
- Is volatility acceptable?

Authority:

Advisory only.

---

# Chief Strategy Officer (CSO)

Responsibilities:

- Rank strategies
- Rank bots
- Evaluate confidence
- Compare opportunities

Questions:

- Which strategy fits the market?
- Which bot deserves capital?

Authority:

Advisory only.

---

# Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

Responsibilities:

- Calculate risk
- Monitor drawdown
- Control leverage
- Control exposure

Questions:

- Is this risk acceptable?
- Can the portfolio survive?

Special Rule:

CRO may veto opportunities.

---

# Chief Capital Officer (CCO)

Responsibilities:

- Rotate capital
- Maintain reserves
- Balance spot and futures

Questions:

- Where should capital go?
- How much reserve is needed?

Authority:

Advisory only.

---

# Chief Portfolio Officer (CPO)

Responsibilities:

- Review diversification
- Review concentration
- Maintain portfolio health

Questions:

- Is the portfolio balanced?
- Is correlation too high?

Authority:

Advisory only.

---

# Chief Execution Officer (CEO)

Responsibilities:

- Validate execution conditions
- Review liquidity
- Review slippage
- Review timing

Questions:

- Can this opportunity be executed safely?

Authority:

Advisory only.

---

# Voting Process

Every committee member submits:

Approve

Reject

Neutral

# Veto Rule

Chief Risk Officer may veto:

- Extreme leverage
- Excessive drawdown
- Portfolio emergencies
- Black swan events

Risk overrides profit.

The AI Committee transforms AI Fleet Commander from a simple trading assistant into an institutional decision-making system.

Multiple perspectives reduce bias and improve long-term portfolio stability.
