# AI Fleet Commander
# Portfolio Manager

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Portfolio Manager is the central decision-making layer of AI Fleet Commander.

It receives information from all engines and converts intelligence into portfolio actions.

The Portfolio Manager never generates trading signals.

Its responsibility is portfolio optimization.

---

# Philosophy

Protect the portfolio.

Allocate capital intelligently.

Adapt continuously.

Think long term.

---

# Responsibilities

The Portfolio Manager continuously:

- Reviews market conditions
- Reviews bot rankings
- Reviews portfolio risk
- Reviews active opportunities
- Reviews capital distribution
- Maintains diversification
- Approves allocations
- Controls reserve capital

---

# Inputs

The Portfolio Manager receives data from:

- Market Brain
- Market Regime Engine
- Strategy Engine
- Bot Ranking Engine
- Capital Rotation Engine
- Risk Engine
- Opportunity Engine
- Bot 11 Advisor

---

# Decision Flow

Market Analysis

Strategy Evaluation

↓

Bot Ranking

↓

Risk Review

Capital Review

Portfolio Review

Final Decision

---

# Core Decisions

The Portfolio Manager decides:

- Which bots receive capital
- How much capital each bot receives
- Which opportunities are approved
- Which opportunities are rejected
- How much reserve capital remains

---

# Portfolio States

The portfolio may operate in:

- Aggressive Mode
- Balanced Mode
- Defensive Mode
- Recovery Mode
- Emergency Mode

---

# Aggressive Mode

Requirements:

- Strong market quality
- High confidence
- Controlled risk

Capital Usage:

80–95%

---

# Balanced Mode

Requirements:

- Normal market conditions

Capital Usage:

60–80%

---

# Defensive Mode

Requirements:

- High uncertainty
- Elevated volatility

Capital Usage:

30–60%

---

# Recovery Mode

Requirements:

- Portfolio drawdown
- Weak performance

Capital Usage:

20–40%

---

# Emergency Mode

Requirements:

- Black swan event
- Exchange failure
- Flash crash

Capital Usage:

0–20%

---

# Diversification Rules

Never concentrate:

- One bot
- One coin
- One strategy
- One market direction

---

# Reserve Rules

Minimum reserve:

10%

Maximum reserve:

40%

Reserve increases automatically during uncertainty.

---

# Approval Rules

A trade is approved only if:

- Market quality acceptable
- Bot confidence sufficient
- Risk acceptable
- Portfolio balanced
- Capital available

---

# Rejection Rules

Reject immediately if:

- Portfolio heat too high
- Correlation too high
- Risk unacceptable
- Drawdown limit reached
- Emergency mode active

---

# AI Committee

Portfolio Manager receives recommendations from:

- Chief Market Officer
- Chief Strategy Officer
- Chief Risk Officer
- Chief Capital Officer
- Bot 11 Advisor

Final authority remains with Fleet Commander.

---

# Final Rule

The portfolio is more important than any individual trade.

Protecting long-term growth always has higher priority than short-term profits.

---

# Conclusion

The Portfolio Manager is the executive layer of AI Fleet Commander.

Its responsibility is to transform market intelligence into intelligent portfolio decisions while protecting capital and maintaining long-term consistency.
